What to try
  • Moons with 3 gold labels per class: compare the gold-only student with the soft-label one. The gold-only student sees 6 points; the others see the shape of the data through the teacher.
  • Set the teacher quality to 1. The students inherit its mistakes: they keep agreeing with the teacher while their test accuracy falls with its own.
  • With a weak teacher, raise lambda: the gold labels pull the students toward the truth near the gold points.
  • Set unlabelled points to 0, then raise it: the distilled students learn from far more points than were labelled by hand.
  • Hover near a boundary: where the teacher is unsure, the soft label keeps that; the hard label turns it into a certain answer. With 3 or 4 noisy blobs the soft labels also say which classes are close.
  • Spiral with one hidden layer of 2 neurons: a student too small for the task gets it wrong whatever the teacher says.
